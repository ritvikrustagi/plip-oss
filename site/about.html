About Jade

Help that holds back the answer.

We’re building the study companion we wanted in school: close enough to see the work, disciplined enough not to do it.

What we believe

A hint is worth more than an answer.

Students aren’t cheating because they’re lazy. They’re stuck at 10pm with nobody to ask, and there is now something that will finish the problem for them in four seconds.

The answer isn’t to block it. It’s to be better than it — to be the thing that knows what the teacher taught, asks the question that moves the student one step, and then tells the teacher what it saw. That’s the whole product.

How we work

Teachers first, then everything else.

Three rules we don’t break, even when breaking one would make a demo look better.

01

No finished answers

Jade gives a question, a step, or a different example. If a student wants the answer handed over, there are plenty of other tabs.

02

The teacher owns the context

Guidance comes from the syllabus and notes a teacher uploaded, not from the internet’s best guess at how their course is taught.

03

Nothing hidden from the school

Every hint a student receives is visible to their teacher. There is no private channel between Jade and a student.
