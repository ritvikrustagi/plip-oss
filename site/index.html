Questions

The things people ask us first.

How is this different from ChatGPT?
Two things. Jade can see the page the student is on, so there is nothing to paste and no room to describe the problem wrongly. And it is built not to finish the work — it gives a question, a step or a different example, then waits. A general chatbot does the opposite on both counts.
What does the teacher actually see?
Every hint a student received, the topic it was about, and whether the student marked it helpful. Grouped by skill so you can spot eleven people stuck on the same prerequisite. It arrives the same minute, not in a weekly digest.
Is there a private channel between Jade and a student?
No. Everything a student is told is visible to their teacher. That rule is why schools let us in, and we don’t make exceptions to it.
What subjects does it cover?
Anything the teacher uploads materials for. Pilots so far have run in algebra and chemistry; the guidance quality tracks how good the course materials are, which is why we start with one teacher rather than a whole school.
What does it cost?
A per-seat annual licence to the school or district, priced below an hour of human tutoring per student per year. A teacher’s first class is free, with no end date on it.
Can we see the pilot data?
Yes. We’ll walk you through a live dashboard rather than a slide, and we’ll show you the sessions that went badly as well as the ones that went well.

Still have a question?

We’ll answer it properly, and we won’t put you in a sequence.
