01 — What we do

Homework help got good
at answers. Nobody got
better at learning.

A chatbot finishes the problem. The student still can’t do the next one, and the teacher finds out a week later, from a grade.

For the student

Jade reads the page they’re actually on — the problem, the diagram, the half-finished work — and gives a hint, not a solution. A guiding question. A different example. The thinking stays theirs.

For the teacher

Every hint Jade gives is grounded in the teacher’s own syllabus and lesson notes, and every moment of confusion lands on a dashboard. The task, the hint, whether it helped.

05 — Why now

Every student
already has an AI.
No teacher has one.

The homework habit changed in about eighteen months, and schools got the worst of the deal: answers arrive instantly, and the signal a teacher used to read off a worksheet is gone.

Blocking it doesn’t work. Jade takes the thing students are already doing and makes it legible — help that holds back the answer, and a record of where the class actually struggled. That record is the part no one else is building, and it’s the part a school will pay for.
