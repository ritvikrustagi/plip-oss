Plip Study Buddy

A study buddy for one browser tab at a time. It helps you reason through your own work. It cannot type into a page, fill a field, press a key or submit anything, by design.

Who you are here

Plip uses a random id, not your name or your email. Nothing here identifies you unless your school maps the id back to you on their own side.

Sharing with your teacher

Off by default. When it is on, events are marked shareable and go into the export file below. Nothing is uploaded by this extension: you or your school move the file. What an event holds: counts (tasks, hints, attempts), an outcome you reported yourself, how long a task took, and concept ids you confirmed. What it never holds: page text, URLs, selections, what you typed to Plip, or any screenshot.

Plip cannot measure attention, effort, mastery or a grade, and nothing it exports should be read as any of those.

The model

A model key never belongs in a browser. The proxy holds it, checks who is asking, and streams text back. If you paste something starting with sk-ant- here, Plip refuses it.

Voice

Off by default, and never required: typing works for everything and never sends audio anywhere.

  • Through your school’s server: Plip records a clip while you hold the microphone, posts it once to the URL above, and shows you the words. Your school’s server holds the speech provider’s key (the reference server uses Deepgram); this extension never has one and will refuse to post audio straight to a speech company.
  • Chrome’s own recogniser: no server to run, but Chrome sends your audio to Google.

Chrome asks for the microphone on this page rather than in the side panel. Check it here once and the panel can use it afterwards.

Your data

Anything older is dropped automatically, every time Plip reads or writes.

Sites Plip may read

    One site at a time, granted by you from the panel. Plip asks Chrome for that one origin and nothing else; it never asks for all sites. Chrome’s own pages, the Web Store, and sign-in, payment and password pages are refused even if a grant exists.

    What Plip cannot do here

    This is a browser extension, so most of what Plip does on a Mac is simply not possible. No native helper changes that, and this extension does not have one.

    School use

    Student-facing software in a school needs a decision from the school: who consents, for which pupils, under which policy. This preview is built so those answers are possible to implement (opt-in sessions, pseudonymous ids, short retention, export and delete, no hidden collection), and it makes no claim of FERPA, COPPA or GDPR compliance. It has not been reviewed or certified by anyone, and it is not published on the Chrome Web Store. Demo data only.
